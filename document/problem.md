# CreditSense AI — Problem Statement

## The Problem in One Sentence

**Millions of creditworthy Pakistanis cannot get loans simply because they have no "paper trail" a traditional bank can read — even though they clearly have the money and ability to repay.**

---

## 1. The Situation Today

Pakistan has made real progress in getting people **bank accounts**, but having an account is **not** the same as getting a loan.

| Years | Account ownership (adults) |
|-------|----------------------------|
| 2015  | ~16%                       |
| 2023  | ~64%                       |
| 2028 (target) | 75%                  |

But even with an account, **two big gaps** remain:

1. **Account ≠ Credit access** — Millions of account holders have *never* taken a formal loan, so they have **no credit history** at all.
2. **Informal workers are invisible to banks** — Freelancers, small shopkeepers, online sellers, drivers, and informal workers earn real money, but it doesn't appear as a payslip a traditional underwriter can check.

---

## 2. The Core Issue: The "Thin-File" Problem

A person can be **financially active and genuinely creditworthy**, yet **invisible** to a scoring system built only around:

- Formal salary slips
- Credit bureau records

Meanwhile, these same people generate **real digital signals every single day** that banks simply ignore:

- Mobile wallet transactions (JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Raast)
- Mobile top-up payments
- Utility bill payments

> **In short:** Real people, real money, real ability to repay — but no way for a lender to *see* it, *trust* it, or *price* it.

---

## 3. Problem Flow (It's Broken Today)

```
   A CREDITWORTHY PERSON
   (freelancer / shopkeeper / driver)
              │
              │ earns steadily, pays bills on time
              ▼
   Has NO formal bank loan history
              │
              ▼
   Traditional bank check fails
   ("no payslip, no credit file")
              │
              ▼
   ❌ LOAN REJECTED  →  person stays locked out
```

---

## 4. What CreditSense AI Solves

CreditSense AI turns those ignored **digital signals** into a **trusted, explainable credit decision**.

| Before (broken) | After (with CreditSense) |
|-----------------|--------------------------|
| Info worker has no credit record | Gets a real risk score from wallet/bill/payment data |
| Bank can't judge ability to repay | Sees repayment probability + affordability |
| Fraud slips through the cracks | Fraud & network risk are flagged |
| No way to track a loan after approval | Continuous monitoring catches early warning signs |

---

## 5. The Four Key Questions CreditSense Answers

| # | Question | Module That Answers It |
|---|----------|------------------------|
| 1 | **Can this person repay?** | CreditSense Score (credit risk) |
| 2 | **How much can they safely borrow?** | Financial Affordability Engine |
| 3 | **Can we trust this application?** | FraudSense (fraud / network risk) |
| 4 | **What happens after the loan is given?** | Continuous Financial Monitoring |

---

## 6. How the Flow Works With CreditSense

```
     APPLICANT (informal worker)
              │
              ▼
   Digital signals collected
   (wallets, top-ups, bills)
              │
              ▼
   CreditSense AI scores them
   (repay probability + 0–1000 score)
              │
              ▼
   Affordability check
   (how much they can safely borrow)
              │
     ┌────────┴────────┐
     ▼                 ▼
  Fraud check      Continuous monitoring
  (trust / risk)   (post-loan early warning)
              │
              ▼
   ✅ LENDER APPROVES WITH CONFIDENCE
```

---

## 7. Who Uses It (Roles)

| Role | What They Do |
|------|--------------|
| **Loan Officer** | Reviews applications, risk scores & plain-language explanations; approves, rejects, or sends to manual review |
| **Risk Analyst** | Views portfolio-level risk distribution, trends, and early-warning signals across all customers |
| **Fraud Analyst** | Investigates flagged applicants & reviews the relationship graph for suspicious clusters |
| **Administrator** | Manages users, permissions, and system configuration |
| **End Customer** | The applicant — doesn't use the dashboard directly; interacts only through the lender's own app |

---

## 8. Who Would Pay For This (Target Customers)

- Commercial banks running digital lending pilots
- Microfinance institutions (Kashf-style, Finja-style lenders)
- Digital lenders & BNPL / consumer finance apps
- FinTech companies building lending features into wallets
- SME lenders

---

## 9. The Bottom Line (What It Solves)

CreditSense AI gives lenders the ability to **say "yes" with confidence** to millions of hardworking Pakistanis who are currently locked out of credit — while **protecting the lender** from risk and fraud. It turns financial *behavior* into **trusted credit**.
